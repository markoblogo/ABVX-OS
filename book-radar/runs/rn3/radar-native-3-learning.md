# Radar-Native #3 learning

- Multilingual discovery produced the strongest opportunity: a Ukrainian-language product for buyers on Amazon.fr. The advantage came from a language/market pair, not translation of an English bestseller.
- Language and marketplace should become permanent first-class fields. RU/UK demand on Amazon.fr and PL demand on Amazon.pl cannot be represented accurately by language alone.
- `COMMERCIAL_DIFFERENTIATION` should enter the v0.2 calibration set. It removed markets where demand was real but the proposed paid product was merely another summary.
- `FREE_ALTERNATIVE_PRESSURE` was predictive only when paired with a concrete paid-value layer. High pressure did not kill RN3-091 because offline bilingual visual decoding is distinct from free rules and apps.
- Portfolio Similarity belongs before deep scan. Eighteen candidates were checked before ranking; adjacent caregiver/emergency organizers were not allowed to become silent duplicates.
- RN2-037 confirms that production feasibility should be driven by Anton-hours, deterministic QA, rights/currentness and rework risk, not page count. RN3 scoring retained that interpretation.
- Of 180 broad hypotheses, 18 entered deep scan and five survived all lightweight gates: a 90% broad-screen rejection rate and a 72% deep-gate exclusion rate. Early gates prevented false winners before final ranking.
- BE produced no credible finalist because observable Amazon evidence was weak. That absence is evidence, not a reason to manufacture quota candidates.

Recommendation: add language, marketplace, commercial differentiation, free-alternative pressure, paid-value layer, author dependency and language-QA cost to Radar v0.2 data. Calibrate weights only after launch actuals; do not change v0.1 weights from this run alone.
