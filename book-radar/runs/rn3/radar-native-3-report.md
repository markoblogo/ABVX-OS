# Radar-Native Discovery #3 - multilingual

Observed September 6, 2026. This run screened 180 buyer markets across EN, FR, RU, UK, BE and PL, then deep-scanned 18. Language and marketplace were recorded separately. Broad-screen evidence was intentionally cheap; only gate survivors received investment-level review.

## Decision

**Winner: RN3-091 - French Road Signs in Ukrainian: A Visual Vocabulary Atlas.**

The market is directly observable: an independently published Ukrainian two-volume French-road-code guide launched in May 2025. Its second volume is EUR17.20, 147 pages, BSR 207,359 and has nine reviews; volume one displays ten. This is meaningful newcomer traction in a narrow language/market pair, without a huge review moat.

The proposed product does not repeat that prose guide. It is a sign-first visual decoding system: official French term, plain Ukrainian meaning, required action, common confusion and spaced visual review. The buyer pays for fast bilingual recognition, not for access to rules that are free online.

The controlling source set can be authoritative and current: the consolidated French road code and signaling order, official Sécurité routière taxonomy, and explicitly Open Licence 2.0 government data. Commercial reuse under that licence requires source and update-date attribution. Competitor text, proprietary exam questions and partner-created government-site illustrations are excluded.

The representative three-card prototype passed expected-label, duplication and collision checks. It is a direction proof, not book production. Final production remains subject to the frozen contract's rights, color-economics, source-crosswalk and Ukrainian sample gates.

Exact score fields, economics, gate results, evidence limits and rejections are in the structured run artifacts. Estimates are hypotheses, not sales forecasts. No Belarusian candidate had enough current Amazon evidence to survive final ranking. English demand was abundant but competition was materially worse. French, Ukrainian and Polish showed the clearest demand/competition asymmetries.

## Stop condition

RN3-091 is production-authorized but production has not started. No manuscript, final cover, upload or listing change was made.
