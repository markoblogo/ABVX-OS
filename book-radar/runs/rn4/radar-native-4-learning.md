# Radar-Native #4 learning

## What changed after RN3

Early gates reduced false positives, but they also exposed a recurring weakness: a plausible buyer job plus an attractive workbook architecture is not enough. Direct newcomer traction remains the scarce evidence. RN4 correctly returned NO PRODUCT instead of converting production feasibility into a demand signal.

## Multilingual search

Language-first exploration still finds less crowded shelves, but the evidence quality falls faster than competition. PL and UK candidates often lacked visible entrant traction or required native review. A non-English gap should therefore raise the evidence requirement, not lower it.

## Portfolio information gain

The field was useful as a tie-breaker. Event workbooks, art workbooks and professional compliance packs would each test a new hypothesis. None deserved selection once its controlling commercial or production gate failed. It should remain experimental and outside permanent scoring weights.

## Product quality risk

The field was highly predictive. Visual diagnostic, activity and color-workbook concepts repeatedly looked easy at outline level while requiring original assets, specialist judgment or physical interaction design. Representative Product Gates remain necessary, but should occur only after commercial evidence survives.

## Rework economics

RN3 showed that page count is a poor proxy for effort: typography, index integrity, bilingual comparison and editorial formalism created the rework. Radar should eventually model expected correction cost as a separate economic term, but RN4 does not change weights. For now, `PRODUCT_QUALITY_RISK` plus `EXPECTED_CORRECTION_MINUTES` should remain explicit evidence.

## Repeated formalism risks

Three classes repeatedly invite formally correct but commercially weak output:

- generic planners/logbooks built from repeated templates;
- visual atlases built from inventories without a decision or transfer model;
- compliance workbooks that restate free official material without a buyer-specific evidence workflow.

## Next Radar change to consider

Add a reusable `NEWCOMER_TRACTION_CONFIDENCE` gate that distinguishes direct BSR/review/date observations from result-count inference. Do not add it during RN4 and do not alter scoring weights until more runs provide calibration data.
