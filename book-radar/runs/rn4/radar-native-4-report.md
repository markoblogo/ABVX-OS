# Radar-Native Discovery #4

Observed September 7, 2026. RN4 screened 200 distinct buyer markets across professional reference, family utility, hobbies, education, activity, language, careers, business, home and gift products. Language and marketplace were first-class fields. Eighteen candidates received a deeper gate review.

## Decision

**NO PRODUCT.**

All five finalists passed the early portfolio-similarity check, but each failed a controlling investment gate:

1. **RN4-001 — Wedding Speech Builder (EN/Amazon.com).** The buyer job is real, but the live search is being flooded by near-identical new workbooks: at least five appeared within eleven days, with no visible rating traction. A differentiated rehearsal workflow would still enter a copycat-heavy shelf without newcomer proof.
2. **RN4-006 — Eulogy Writing Workbook (FR/Amazon.fr).** The event-triggered job and workbook format are coherent, but current sales evidence was not observable and a credible incumbent is explicitly professional-led. Trust cannot be replaced by generic AI packaging.
3. **RN4-011 — Home Construction Control Book (PL/Amazon.pl).** The proposed change-order/decision-trail architecture is stronger than a blank journal. However, the current French analogue has 61 results, low visible rating counts and a EUR5.99-EUR12.16 price cluster. Earlier RN3 evidence no longer supports a high-price, weak-competition assumption strongly enough.
4. **RN4-096 — VSME Sustainability Data Pack (UK/Amazon.fr).** The institutional buyer pain is real, but the relevant EU standard changed in July 2026, free official templates are available, and no exact Amazon workbook traction was observed. This is a consulting/software channel before it is a KDP opportunity.
5. **RN4-182 — Watercolor Mixing Recipes (FR/Amazon.fr).** Buyers do pay for guided color practice, but the product depends on expensive color printing, original high-quality assets and human art-direction. No recent newcomer evidence justified that production risk.

The autonomous selection loop tested all five in rank order and stopped at NO PRODUCT. No production contract was frozen because no candidate survived. No representative sample was created: a sample can test product quality, but cannot repair missing commercial evidence.

## Evidence interpretation

Amazon result counts, prices and ratings are direct observations, not sales estimates. BSR was not visible on the inspected result pages and was not invented. Third-party niche lists were used only to generate ideas. Current regulated facts were checked against European Commission and EFRAG sources, not competitor books.

The structured broad map, deep scan, rejection history, gate outcomes, similarity audit and source ledger are stored beside this report. The 90-day EV values in the deep scan are deliberately labeled hypotheses; they are not forecasts and did not override failed gates.

## Stop condition

RN4 discovery is complete. Production authorization is **NO** and production has not started.
