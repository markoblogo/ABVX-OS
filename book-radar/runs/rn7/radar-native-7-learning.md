# Radar-Native #7 learning

RN7 tested `candidate-generation-v2` out of sample with 360 fresh opportunities: 270 commercial-intent and 90 exploratory. Promotion volume remained nearly unchanged (8.3% broad-to-deep versus 8.5% across RN4–RN6), so recall was not mechanically crushed.

One candidate reached conditional authorization. RN7-001 has a time-bound, high-value transaction, a mandatory document, a natural print workflow, authoritative sources, a weak exact Amazon shelf and a concrete transformation that does more than summarize regulation. It is still conditional because adjacent newcomer traction is modest and the product must prove it can organize without impersonating a surveyor or lawyer.

The average and median commercial-intent confidence among deep candidates are stored in `calibration-comparison.json`. The result is directionally encouraging, not causal proof: a single run cannot validate new features or justify permanent score changes.

The exploratory path remained active and supplied 25% of the broad map. No permanent weights changed.
