# RN9 learning

The useful unit of discovery is not a topic or search result. It is a dated observation tied to a buyer, trigger, paid-book behavior and specific scenario.

Three changes should persist:

1. Require purchase evidence, scenario and accessible gap as separate fields. A trend, listing or request cannot substitute for all three.
2. Count independent origins, not URLs. Syndicated listings and repeated discussion of one buyer event are one origin.
3. Apply rights, expertise and timing before expensive competitor work. A commercially interesting problem can still be `ATTRACTIVE_BUT_NOT_FOR_US`.

Source-family learning: recent purchaser reviews were most diagnostic; direct requests were best for scenario language but weak for willingness to pay; specialist markets established format and substitute baselines; event sources were almost useless without separate purchase evidence.
