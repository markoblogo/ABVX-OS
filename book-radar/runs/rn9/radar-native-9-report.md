# Radar-Native Discovery #9

## Decision

`RN9-001 — RV Maintenance Evidence Book` is an `EVIDENCE_BACKED_CONCEPT`, not a production authorization. `RN9-002 — Self-Fitting Triage Workbook for Sewists` remains `WATCH`. The run screened 9 evidence cards, deep-checked 5 and retained 2; it did not pad to the allowed limits.

## Mechanism deficiency fixed

RN7 and RN8 could assemble persuasive concepts before proving three separate foundations: an observed book purchase, a narrow buyer scenario and an accessible gap. Several URLs could also repeat one underlying origin. The revised strategy now requires a dated evidence card, an alternative explanation and independent-origin keys, then stops a candidate before deep scan when purchase evidence, gap access, rights, expertise or timing fails.

## Strongest concept

The RV product is not another blank log and not a repair handbook. It teaches a new owner to extract intervals from their own motorhome, chassis and appliance manuals; record the source; track completed work, receipts, warranty issues and recall checks; and generate questions for a dealer or service provider. Current purchaser reviews support the physical record-keeping job and expose the gap: products described as mainly blank or too small do not help the owner build a defensible evidence trail.

The core uncertainty is price elasticity. Free templates and apps are credible substitutes, so the next gate must prove that the guided extraction workflow earns a premium before manuscript production.

## Rejections

- Allotment: real beginner demand, but an accessible advantage over trusted RHS and bestselling incumbents was not established.
- CDL pre-trip: real verbal-rehearsal pain, but current book traction, state scope and autonomous QA remain weak.
- Genealogy: paid niche evidence exists, but the narrow job is already served by specialist paid and free authority materials.
- macOS 27 and Rugby World Cup 2027: confirmed events without sufficient buyer-and-gap evidence.
- Birding: attractive physical format, but image rights and specialist burden fail the autonomous-production boundary.
- Espresso: workflow requests point mainly to free community and app solutions, not book purchase.

## Portfolio boundary

The portfolio registry was used only to block duplication. No sales analysis, repackaging audit, price change or schedule change was performed.
