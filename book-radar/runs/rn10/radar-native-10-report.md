# Radar-Native Discovery #10

## Decision

RN10 validates the commercial-first v5 mechanism and retains two market concepts for premise validation. Production remains unauthorized.

1. **RN10-002 — Cozy Town-Building LitRPG** ranks first on commercial evidence. It carries high production effort and series risk.
2. **RN10-001 — Later-in-Life Contemporary Romance** ranks second. It has moderate commercial evidence and medium production effort.

The run screened nine market cards and deep-checked five. It did not generate a compulsory practical tool, workbook or novel mechanism.

## What changed

New discovery starts with readership, reading motivation, market, promise and channel. Fiction, business, popular nonfiction, hobbies, children's/education, gift/visual books and practical reference are eligible. Commercial potential is the primary criterion. Production effort breaks ties inside an evidence tier and cannot rescue weak demand.

Reader choice may come from genre experience, voice, subject, identity, curation, design or explanation. A missing utilitarian task is no longer required. Fiction still has a real quality cost: narrative, genre satisfaction, editing, cover and series continuity count toward production effort.

## Market findings

- Romance is a large active market with documented self-published entry, but newcomer outcomes are highly selective.
- LitRPG has unusually clear indie-native distribution through Kindle, Kindle Unlimited and audio. Softer progression has current reader-request evidence.
- Children's activity books have stronger measured category growth than either finalist, but licensing, visual work and testing weaken the current entry case.
- Coloring and screen-free hobbies deserve monitoring, but production ease and newcomer reach were not demonstrated.
- AI-agent business books are timely supply, not yet observed purchase evidence for a new low-authority entrant.
- Manga growth is strong but outside the current low-cost production boundary.

## Evidence limits

Industry category totals do not forecast a title. Bestseller pages, recommendations and reviews are proxies, not unit sales. Successful indie-to-traditional deals establish possibility but not the base rate. No Amazon impression data, paid BookScan title data or advertising CAC were available. Consequently both finalists require premise and positioning validation before any manuscript decision.

## Post-run user-supplied concept: RN10-003

RN10-003, *Good Dog, Bad System*, is recorded separately from the nine scanned market cards. It combines the user-owned “Собакокалипсис” premise with twelve substantially transformed situation prompts from a second supplied source. Its working position is a dog-POV, darkly comic system-apocalypse and civilization-building LitRPG.

The tonal sequence is deliberate: a sincerely cozy opening with lovable dogs and affectionate owner routines, an uncanny System shift, then black comedy as care becomes dependency and canine settlement mechanics become political power. The package must expose both the cozy entry and darker destination so readers do not mistake it for pure cozy pet fiction.

The concept gate includes a first-volume synopsis, five-stat progression system, twelve-beat map, three-book first-season arc and provisional Amazon metadata. The unavailable reader-panel gate was replaced by a dated Amazon listing-pattern scan and later real-market observation. Status: `LISTING_PATTERN_GATE_PASS`. Production remains unauthorized; the next optional gate is outline and sample development.
