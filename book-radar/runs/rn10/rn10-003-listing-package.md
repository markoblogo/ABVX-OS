## Description

Mriya was supposed to find one buried package. Instead, she found the System.

Humans cannot see its menus. Dogs can.

Every hidden Cache offers experience. Every city block can become territory. Every human bond can unlock food, shelter, medicine—or something much worse. When an aging shelter mutt chooses neither Good Dog nor Bad Dog, she unlocks the forbidden Stray class and accidentally founds the city’s first canine settlement.

Now Mriya must turn a ruined service tunnel into a home, unite rival dogs and manage the unstable humans who think they are still in charge. But each new facility makes the pack stronger and humanity more dependent. Somewhere beneath the city, a tiny administrator is measuring whether people are useful enough to keep.

For the dogs, it is civilization building. For everyone else, it is the apocalypse.

A darkly comic, dog-POV LitRPG with territory progression, settlement building, pack politics and no harem.

## Cover brief

Foreground an appealing but alert shelter dog inside a warm domestic pool of light. Behind or below that cozy first read, reveal a ruined contemporary city, scent trails and restrained interface glyphs. At thumbnail size the cover should first say “charming dog adventure,” then “dark comic apocalypse.”

Use a dark palette with one toxic-neon accent and a large, legible title. Avoid gore, a purely cute cartoon treatment and cluttered game-interface panels. The contrast belongs in the image: the dog remains lovable while the world around her becomes alarming.
