## Positioning

- **Working title:** *Good Dog, Bad System*
- **Series:** *Good Dogs of the Apocalypse*, Book 1
- **Subtitle:** *A Darkly Comic System Apocalypse LitRPG*
- **Hook:** **Cozy for the dogs. Apocalypse for everyone else.**
- **Genre:** dog-POV system apocalypse, civilization building, black comedy

The opening is sincerely cozy: Mriya is an aging shelter mutt learning the rituals of a safe home with Nika. The contrast works because affection is real. Treats, obedience, protection and dependence then acquire System values and become instruments of power. The book should not flatten that arc by presenting the dogs as sinister from page one or by promising pure cozy fantasy.

## First-volume synopsis

Mriya begins in a warm world of blankets, treats and small rituals with Nika, a burned-out courier who takes her home. Following a strange scent into a service tunnel, she finds a hidden package and a System interface no human can see. The System classifies intentionally hidden objects as Caches, converts canine instincts into progression and allows dogs to claim neglected city spaces.

Mriya wants only to protect Nika and preserve their new domestic life. Instead, every useful upgrade makes humans more dependent on the pack. After rejecting the System’s Good Dog and Bad Dog choices, she unlocks the rare Stray class. She recruits rival dogs, turns a ruined utility room into the first Pack Hearth and inherits three incompatible human followers.

A failed settlement build, species-specific consumables and a trusted human who returns with the wrong scent reveal that the System is testing more than survival. In the finale, Mriya bargains with a hidden administrator for a provisional territory charter. The Pack Hearth survives and the first-book objective closes, while the final reveal shows canine interfaces awakening across the city.

## Progression

The core loop is: **find a Cache → choose how to use it → earn Trust, Supply or Territory → recruit → build → absorb the consequence**.

Five stats keep the system readable:

1. **Scent** — find traces and infer their origin.
2. **Bond** — understand and influence humans.
3. **Pack** — recruit, coordinate and lead dogs.
4. **Territory** — claim, build and defend places.
5. **Adaptation** — resist toxins, environments and anomalies.

Mriya’s Stray class rewards negotiated loyalty, mixed-species settlements and solutions that break the System’s binary rules. An upgrade counts only when it changes a relationship, a place or a strategic choice.

## First season

1. Found and defend the first Pack Hearth.
2. Build a self-sustaining canine district before rival packs absorb it.
3. Decide whether city-scale canine rule protects humanity or owns it.

The third book can bring the essay’s Plan Barbossa logic into open political conflict. Planning beyond this three-book season would be false precision before the first package passes validation.
