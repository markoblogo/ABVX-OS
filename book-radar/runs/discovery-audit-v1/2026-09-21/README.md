# Amazon Discovery Audit v1 — 2026-09-21

Read-only marketplace snapshots collected after the first launch windows. They are intentionally additive: they do not amend historical opportunity scores, KDP records, or listing metadata.

`amazon.com` observations were made in an authenticated, personalized shopper context with international delivery settings. `amazon.fr` observations were made while signed out. Exact rank is therefore never inferred from the snapshots.
