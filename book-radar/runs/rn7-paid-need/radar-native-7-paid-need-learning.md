# RN7 paid-need learning

Starting from paid situations changed the failure timing. RV, HOA, septic and financial-aid concepts were rejected before product work because current books or free tools already capture the proposed outcome. Horse, tree-risk, food-truck, marine and art candidates were separated as `ATTRACTIVE_BUT_NOT_FOR_US` instead of being mislabeled as no demand.

Amazon/book discovery was most useful for format evidence and competitive falsification. Service-spend discovery was most useful for proving the problem and buyer value, but regularly failed to prove book demand. Unmet-need discovery produced useful prompts but the weakest commercial proof.

One ready-for-validation hypothesis is not evidence that the new method outperforms RN6. RN6 did not store comparable spending and format fields, and sample sizes differ. Keep the strategy experimental until multiple out-of-sample runs or paid tests produce comparable outcomes.
