# Radar-Native #5 learning

RN4’s zero was primarily a combination of search-space concentration, obvious keyword markets and too many low-content/workbook concepts; the gates behaved appropriately. RN5 kept the gates and diversified the generating architecture.

- Opportunity diversity: 280 markets versus 200, with 70 underlying buyer jobs and 14 architecture families rather than a mostly workbook-shaped shelf scan.
- Gate survival: one of seven finalists reached conditional authorization; RN4 had zero of five.
- Languages: all six requested languages were screened. EN and FR produced credible finalists; RU and BE were stopped as UNVERIFIED at format eligibility, and PL/UK candidates did not beat the evidence bar.
- Discovery origin: RN5 deliberately included externally proven quote, report and decision problems whose exact book shelves were weak.
- Format eligibility: it prevented RU/BE economics from being treated as publishable facts and kept Kindle out of a print-first winner’s thesis.
- False positives: contractor-bid and used-EV ideas looked easy but current Amazon results exposed direct low-traction copycat competition; professional-authority risk blocked home-inspection elevation.
- NO PRODUCT probability: still material. RN5 has only a conditional survivor; a failed human product gate converts the result to NO PRODUCT without lowering thresholds.

Do not change permanent weights. After several launches, compare realized sales with `EXTERNALLY_PROVEN_JOB`, `EXACT_BOOK_GAP`, and `NEWCOMER_TRACTION_CONFIDENCE` before promoting them into scoring.
