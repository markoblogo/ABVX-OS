# Radar-Native Discovery #5

Observed 2026-09-07. RN5 screened 280 buyer markets and deep-scanned 24. The run originated from buyer decisions and external evidence, then checked Amazon—not the reverse.

## Decision

**RN5-001 — Solar Quote Decoder: CONDITIONAL GO — AWAITING HUMAN PRODUCT GATE.**

The thesis is narrow: a U.S. homeowner brings two to four real rooftop-solar or battery proposals and leaves with normalized cash/loan economics, production assumptions, equipment, scope, warranties, exclusions and unresolved questions. It is not a solar installation guide and does not recommend a loan or installer.

The underlying job is real. DOE tells buyers to compare quotes and cost per watt. CFPB documents hidden solar-loan markups and recommends multiple written proposals. EnergySage reports millions of marketplace data points and active quote demand. On Amazon, the exact workbook query produced only one tangential result, while recent adjacent homeowner solar guides showed visible traction. This is evidence of a proven problem with a weak exact book solution, though exact-workbook demand remains the main uncertainty.

The proposed transformation is: **buyer proposals → normalized cash/loan, equipment, assumptions, warranty and exclusion fields → an apples-to-apples sign-or-question decision packet.**

Format eligibility passes for English paperback. Kindle is supported but not commercially justified for a writable side-by-side workbook. Portfolio similarity is 8/100 and NOVEL against the complete known catalog.

Conservative hypothesis: $16.99 paperback, $6.50 royalty per unit, 30 units in 90 days, $195 EV, 1.5 Anton-hours, $130 per Anton-hour. BSR was not visible and was not invented.

The eight-page representative sample tests the transformation itself. Full production remains blocked until the human product gate passes.

## RN4 to RN5

RN4 concentrated too heavily on obvious Amazon keyword shelves and workbook concepts whose free alternatives or trust requirements erased paid value. Its gates behaved correctly. RN5 broadened discovery toward document-decoding, quote-normalization, cross-system and field-retrieval architectures, originated more candidates from external buyer problems, and applied format eligibility before economics. This reduced format waste and produced one conditional survivor without lowering any gate.

Discovery is complete. Production has not started.
