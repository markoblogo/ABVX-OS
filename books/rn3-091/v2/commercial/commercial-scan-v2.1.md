# RN3-091 V2.1 commercial scan

Research date: 2026-09-07. Primary marketplace: Amazon.fr. Amazon observations are point-in-time signals, not factual authorities for the book.

## Search-intent findings

- `code de la route ukrainien` returned only generic French exam-prep advertising in the visible Amazon.fr book results; no clearly matching Ukrainian-language France road-rule guide appeared.
- The Ukrainian query `правила дорожнього руху франція` returned no Amazon.fr Books results.
- `panneaux de signalisation livre` produced mostly quiz cards, children's colouring books and weak literal sign books rather than an adult FR↔UA transfer guide.
- The mainstream `Code Rousseau de la route B 2026` results are active and price-sensitive. Current visible paperback prices clustered at €9.20–€19.90, with major/current offers at €9.90, €13.90, €14.90, €16.30 and €19.90.

## Comparable products observed

- *CODE DE LA ROUTE ROUSSEAU B 2026*: paperback, 256 pages, €16.30, published 9 July 2025, 335 ratings, #515 Books and #1 in both Transport and Code de la route when checked. Its promise is French exam preparation, explanations, diagrams and online practice.
- *Code Rousseau de la route B 2027*: paperback, €19.90, published 12 August 2026, 1 rating when checked.
- *Code de la route 2027* (Activ permis): paperback, €9.90, published 1 July 2026, 11 ratings when checked.
- *Code de la Route 2026-2027* (Permis AZE): paperback €14.99 and Kindle €5.99, published 27 April 2026, 14 ratings when checked; the promise is exam coverage plus 1,500 online questions.
- *Code de la route 2026: Les pièges à éviter*: paperback, €13.90, recent self-published entrant with visible review traction.
- *Driving Test in France: Code de la Route Guide 2026*: English-language paperback, €15.82, 3 ratings when checked. This confirms a non-French-language bridge product but not a Ukrainian solution.
- *Code de la route: la méthode visuelle*: paperback, €19.97, 36 ratings when checked, showing that a differentiated visual method can hold the upper end of the visible band.

## What the scan means

The market pays for curation, explanation, visual structure, currency and a usable study route—not merely for a sign inventory. RN3-091 should not compete on exam-question volume. Its defensible promise is narrower and clearer: explain the French system in Ukrainian, then show experienced Ukrainian drivers where prior knowledge transfers and where it becomes a trap.

The direct query gap is real but demand volume for Ukrainian phrases is not observable from Amazon alone. Treat this as a niche conversion experiment, not proof of a large market. The cover and description must state “for France” and “Ukrainian” instantly.
