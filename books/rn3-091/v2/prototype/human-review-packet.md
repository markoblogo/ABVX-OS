# RN3-091 V2 — one product-perspective gate

## What changed

V1 was a technically correct bilingual sign atlas. V2 instead uses an experienced Ukrainian driver's existing knowledge and marks safe transfer, near matches, different systems, and concepts without a direct analogue.

## What this prototype demonstrates

- 14 representative pages and all 13 required page types;
- a difference-first opening section;
- France-primary / Ukraine-secondary visual hierarchy;
- MATCH, NEAR MATCH, DIFFERENT SYSTEM and NO DIRECT ANALOGUE states;
- two-up, four-up, teaching, comparison, review and lookup pages.

## What is not being asked

Do not verify laws, sanctions, sign codes or translations as factual authority. Those are mapped to official French and Ukrainian sources in the claim ledger.

## The only gate question

Does this comparison/product experience correspond to the questions and confusion a Ukrainian driver in France actually has?

Full V2 production remains blocked until this question is answered.
