# RN3-091 V2.1 pricing analysis

Research date: 2026-09-07.

## Recommended launch prices

- Paperback on Amazon.fr: **€18.90**.
- Kindle on Amazon.fr: **€7.99**, 70% royalty option.

The paperback sits above mass-market €9.90 exam manuals but below the current €19.90–€19.97 upper band. That premium is justified by 8.5 × 11 colour production, Ukrainian-language scarcity and the original FR↔UA comparison layer. A price above €20 would ask the unproven niche to absorb both a language premium and an unfamiliar imprint premium.

The Kindle price is above the observed €5.99 generic exam guide but remains far below the paperback. The EPUB is image-heavy (about 24.3 MB), so the 70% option's Amazon.fr delivery charge is material; reducing image weight later would improve Kindle economics without changing the list price.

## Current print economics

KDP classifies 8.5 × 11 as large trim. For Amazon.fr standard-colour paperbacks of 72–600 pages, the current formula is €0.75 fixed plus €0.035 per page.

- Page count: 84.
- Printing cost: €0.75 + (84 × €0.035) = **€3.69**.
- Paperback royalty rate at €18.90: 60% (Amazon.fr threshold is €9.99).
- Estimated Amazon.fr royalty: (0.60 × €18.90) − €3.69 = **€7.65 per copy**.
- Break-even/minimum price at 60%: €3.69 ÷ 0.60 = €6.15, but a price below €9.99 moves to the 50% tier; this is not a commercially sensible target.

These are KDP formula estimates. The Rights & Pricing screen remains the controlling pre-publication check because Amazon can update costs and local tax treatment.

## Kindle economics

At approximately 24.3 MB and €0.12/MB on Amazon.fr, delivery cost is approximately €2.92 before KDP's final file-size rounding. With French VAT and delivery cost, expected royalty is roughly **€3.2–€3.4** at €7.99. This is an estimate, not the figure to enter in KDP; KDP calculates it after upload.

## Secondary marketplaces

Set Amazon.fr manually as the primary €18.90/€7.99 market. For the first launch, allow KDP to convert other marketplace prices automatically, then inspect the generated figures before publishing. Suggested manual anchors only if clean psychological pricing is desired after review: Amazon.com $20.99 paperback / $8.99 Kindle; Amazon.co.uk £16.99 paperback / £6.99 Kindle; Amazon.de €18.90 paperback / €7.99 Kindle. Do not let secondary-price polishing delay the France test.
