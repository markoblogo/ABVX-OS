# Your SaaS Bill Is Ridiculous — Book Voice Contract v1

Status: APPROVED
Source: BOOK-FACTORY-005 human decision `APPROVE_WITH_VOICE_CORRECTION`

## Core voice

The prose should be:

- concise
- skeptical
- practical
- intelligent
- mildly irreverent
- dry where useful
- opinionated when evidence supports the opinion
- readable by a technically interested non-specialist

The energy may be Clarkson-like in pace and bluntness, but the wording must remain the owner's own voice.

Do not imitate Jeremy Clarkson's exact phrasing, syntax, catchphrases, or recognizable passages.

## Central rule

Facts are systematic.
Prose is not.

Every repository should answer the same practical questions in substance, but not:

- in the same order
- with the same paragraph count
- with the same opening pattern
- with the same conclusion pattern

## Avoid template repetition

Do not let these become scaffolding:

- "The catch is..."
- "What does it replace?"
- "That means..."
- "The honest reading..."
- "The good news is..."
- "So the verdict is..."
- "In other words..."

Occasional natural use is acceptable. Repeated mechanical use is not.

## Openings

Vary openings aggressively. Useful modes include:

- blunt opinion
- absurd observation
- concrete user problem
- price comparison
- anecdotal framing
- direct warning
- question
- surprising technical fact
- straight description when cleverness would be forced

Do not force every opening to contain a joke.

## Humor

Humor should come from:

- operational absurdity
- pricing
- unnecessary complexity
- inflated marketing claims
- the distance between "free" and the labour required to operate it

Do not add generic jokes simply to sound witty.

## Conclusions

Do not make every ending symmetrical and balanced.

Entries may end:

- decisively
- skeptically
- with a warning
- with a recommendation
- in one short sentence
- directly into the fact strip

## Owner projects

`ABVX Agent Skills` and `AGENTS.md Generator` receive the same skepticism as third-party projects.

Allowed:

- small first-person context only when it materially explains why the tool exists

Not allowed:

- founder promotion
- special praise because the owner created it
- "revolutionary" language

## Length contract

FULL_PAGE entries:

- typical: 250-350 words
- short: 180-250 words
- exceptional: 350-450 words

450 is a ceiling, not a target.

Use the shortest length that explains the tool properly.

## Standard fact strip

Every FULL_PAGE entry ends with:

- REPLACES
- COST
- SETUP
- BEST FOR
- VERDICT
- URL

The fact strip stays standardized even when the prose does not.

## Setup model

Use the smallest accurate setup label, including mixed cases where needed:

- Low
- Medium
- High
- Easy hosted / High self-hosted
- Low local / Medium production

Do not turn setup into a scoring system.

## Verdict vocabulary

Use only:

- SELF-HOST IT
- TRY IT FIRST
- JUST PAY FOR THE SAAS
- ONLY IF YOU ENJOY PAIN

Do not default everything to `TRY IT FIRST`.
