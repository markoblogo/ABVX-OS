# Good Dogs of the Apocalypse continuity authority

`series-continuity-pack.json` is the canonical continuity source for book 1 revisions and every later volume. The series bible explains story meaning; this pack controls dates, geography, progression balances, permissions, debts and carryovers.

Before drafting or revising a later volume:

1. Load this pack and the series bible.
2. Append new events and ledger changes; do not silently overwrite prior states.
3. Use the four canonical Hearth route names.
4. Reconcile Supply, Favor, Bond, alliance charges, Pack and Territory at every volume gate.
5. Record any retcon as a versioned canon migration.
