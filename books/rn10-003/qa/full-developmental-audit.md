## P0 revisions

### 1. Canonical calendar — chapters 1–36

The apartment sequence is carefully timed, but the depot sequence relies on `tonight`, `next morning`, `first light` and System cycles without a stable day count. The Hearth, Bond crisis, four-door expansion, poisoning, Rook politics, counterfeit Nika and charter audit appear to occur within only a few days. Political and recovery developments risk feeling faster than the bodies and infrastructure allow.

**Fix:** create a day-by-day calendar, add no more than one or two explicit time anchors per chapter, and ensure Borscht, Nika, Vale and the repaired water network recover at compatible rates. Preserve urgency; do not add empty days.

### 2. Close-third POV breach — chapter 33

Nika's escape through the fold is narrated as direct fact although the novel otherwise stays close to Mriya. The information is good, but its delivery briefly creates an omniscient camera.

**Fix:** frame the route as Nika's fragmented account, Irena's questions and evidence Mriya can smell. Keep Nika active without entering her unmediated interior.

### 3. LitRPG progression visibility — chapters 12–36

Stray and Chosen Route land strongly, but explicit character progression becomes sparse afterward. Scent +1 and Adaptation +1 do not visibly advance; Pack and Territory awards appear without a stable status snapshot. Governance mechanics grow while the personal build becomes harder to track.

**Fix:** define a canonical progression ledger and add three or four restrained status beats at major gates: Hearth establishment, Bond sacrifice, species law/alliance, and charter. Show costs and changed capabilities. Do not add grinding, XP filler or arbitrary levels.

### 4. External factions and human antagonist arrive late — chapters 13–36

High Collars and Free Teeth are named in chapter 30 and physically pressure the charter almost immediately. The tunnel man drives chapters 3, 9 and 30 and enables the counterfeit threat, then disappears from the ending. The late concentration makes Act IV feel newly populated.

**Fix:** seed two or three earlier signs of courthouse registration, market theft and the tunnel man's institutional objective. Give chapter 36 one concrete consequence or forward hook for the tunnel man without resolving him.

### 5. Reciprocal Duty exception needs earlier rule support — chapters 15, 18, 27, 34–36

The active final test is persuasive, but the hidden fourth stamp can still read as a rule invented at the climax. `Poor standardization / high persistence` and local exceptions appear only near Custodian 0.03.

**Fix:** plant earlier evidence that the System records nonstandard persistent solutions even when it cannot classify them. In the finale, make the provisional exception a consequence of that accumulated record rather than a surprise capability.
