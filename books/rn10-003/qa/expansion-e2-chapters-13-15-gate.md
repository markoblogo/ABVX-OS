## Labor, privacy and consent

- Locker placement, drainage, scarce wash water and Atlas's ambiguous help turn cleaning into negotiated work.
- Every marker carries a distinct history and a real cost. Nika tests lesser substitutes and makes the photograph choice herself while preserving her grievance with Mriya.
- The privacy exception requires two independent recognitions, limits sole-witness access to imminent bodily harm, and restricts disclosure to the necessary route, object or action.
- Pixel's false cracker emergency is rejected. A drain backflow then proves the rule can disclose the correct valve action without exposing private memories.

## Hearth capability contract

- Provides finite clean-water releases, bounded warmth, rest, minor recovery and shared warning.
- Does not generate food, fully heal injuries, identify intent, detect patient deception, widen passages or decide admission.
- Visitors remain outside the benefits unless invited; Atlas can guard without becoming a member.
- The brief domestic calm comes from maintained choices and work. It does not resolve Nika's anger or erase material scarcity.

## External reaction

- The signal produces different observed responses: petition, withdrawal, protection of pups, route marking and organized investigation.
- Mriya must already exclude an unknown water request because the Hearth lacks a safe response channel.
- Rook's party reaches the eastern grille with tools and prior infrastructure knowledge. The boundary identifies them as uninvited but cannot expel or interpret them.
- Mriya answers the territory question with collective possession, preserving the Stray principle and opening the resource-politics block.
