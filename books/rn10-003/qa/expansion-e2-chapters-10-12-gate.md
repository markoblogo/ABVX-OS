## Class evidence

- **Good Dog:** Atlas uses Trusted Command to move dogs and humans before a falling live cable strikes. The skill compresses earned trust, visible danger and trained direction without making refusal impossible.
- **Atlas:** his missing handler and evacuation of eleven trapped dogs explain why service and command are survival rather than submission to him.
- **Bad Dog:** Pixel uses Fear Claim to convert surrendered space into access, recover water and take supplies from a rat-held hatch.
- **Pixel:** hunger training and abandonment explain why recognized violation feels like freedom. She shares claimed resources, proving Bad Dog does not require waste or isolation.
- **Tradeoff:** Trusted Command depends on continuing recognition. Fear Claim preserves abandoned access but cannot create fear by itself and becomes a trap when Pixel tries to hold a flooding hatch against water.

## Refusal cost

Declining classification suspends shaped dog speech, concealment interpretation, claim guidance, Favor access and territory eligibility. Earned Scent +1 and Adaptation +1 remain, but their class-mediated extensions disappear. During the flood Mriya misunderstands Atlas's warning, cannot redirect Pixel, suffers further injury and completes the rescue without enhanced coordination. Both offered classes remain available throughout.

## Stray trigger

Mriya rejects a single-command crossing and an unknown Fear Claim route. She instead:

1. returns the medicine case to Emil and the copper wire to Pixel;
2. asks Pixel to reach a manual release and Atlas to hold the bridge cable;
3. uses Borscht's observation and waits for Emil's risk decision;
4. crosses the unstable route, then returns for companions free to reject it;
5. crosses last beside Nika.

The System recognizes loyalty offered without command or fear only after this sequence.

## Chosen Route contract

- Shares member position, direction and willingness to continue, never thoughts or private intent.
- Requires a continuous traversable path tested by at least one member.
- Renews when destination, risk or terms materially change.
- Permits immediate withdrawal without punishment; route reach and member-dependent benefits contract at once.
- Cannot locate a non-consenting missing companion or identify safety beyond an untested branch.

## Character and continuity

- Atlas declines membership after receiving a full offer; guarding the departure remains his independent promise.
- Pixel negotiates an emergency exception before joining.
- Borscht's first attempted consent fails until Emil explains the terms and he considers them.
- Emil joins through his reciprocal commitment to Borscht rather than canine authority.
- Stray restores access and adds Chosen Route but does not heal Mriya's hip, nail or flood injuries.
