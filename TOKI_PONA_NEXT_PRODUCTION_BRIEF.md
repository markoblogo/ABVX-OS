# Proposed Production Brief — Jekyll & Hyde in Toki Pona

**Status: proposed only — not authorized for production.** This brief follows candidate selection; it neither translates nor creates assets.

## Product

- **Working title:** *The Strange Case of Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde — Toki Pona Edition with Sitelen Pona*.
- **Title strategy:** keep the globally recognisable English source title first; make the transformation explicit rather than implying a conventional new translation.
- **Reader promise:** a complete, traceable three-layer reading edition of Stevenson's short Gothic classic: named public-domain English reference, Toki Pona rendering, and sitelen pona counterpart, supported by a compact character/name and recurring-anchor guide.
- **Likely buyer:** existing Toki Pona / sitelen pona readers, conlang readers, and collectors of unusual public-domain classic editions. Do not target generic horror readers as the primary buyer.

## Canonical source and rights basis

- **Canonical textual source:** Project Gutenberg eBook 42, *The Strange Case of Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde*, Robert Louis Stevenson: `https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/42`.
- **Rights basis:** the source record labels its English text public domain in the USA. Capture a source snapshot/checksum before production. Do not use a modern introduction, cover, annotations, adaptation, audio script, or modern illustration.
- **Territory gate:** verify intended KDP distribution territory rules and source status before release. Public-domain status of the source is not permission to reuse a later edition's assets.

## Translation and layout scope

1. Complete base text, nine sections, retaining section structure.
2. English source text → Toki Pona rendering → sitelen pona rendering, with stable parallel correspondence at a practical paragraph/block level.
3. Initial note defines this as a new rendering, identifies source edition, and explains the deliberate compression principle.
4. Character/name guide: Jekyll, Hyde, Utterson, Lanyon, Poole, Carew; include recurring places/objects/actions only where navigation needs them.
5. Recurring-anchor guide: identity, secrecy, medicine, letter, door, laboratory, change, fear, law. It is an aid, not interpretation replacing the story.

## Sitelen pona strategy

- Use sitelen as the third readable language layer, not an illustration substitute.
- Add only restrained original structural visuals: identity/character key, section markers and recurring-anchor index. Avoid copied public-domain scans unless their individual provenance is documented.
- Preserve the existing glyph/font licensing evidence. Text must remain selectable/accessible in Kindle where technically possible; fixed layout is acceptable only with device limitations disclosed.

## Formats and production shape

- **Primary:** 6 × 9 in colour or grayscale paperback only after readability/print-cost modelling; likely 150–220 pages depending on the three-layer density.
- **Secondary:** fixed-layout Kindle Print Replica or equivalent, only if device support and price make sense. Do not promise reflowable accessibility if the parallel layout cannot support it.
- **Cover:** separate human gate. Must communicate *Jekyll & Hyde*, `Toki Pona`, and `sitelen pona` at thumbnail size; no film, TV or modern adaptation imagery.

## QA gates

1. source provenance and checksum gate;
2. rights/territory/asset audit;
3. semantic ledger for people, places, repeated objects, chronology and reveal-sensitive terms;
4. independent Toki Pona linguistic/editorial review, including sitelen rendering;
5. English-source alignment diff and missing-block audit;
6. spoiler/revelation audit: navigation material must not reveal more than the source at the relevant section;
7. paperback margins/bleed/font-embedding/preflight and Kindle navigation/TOC/device check;
8. product-quality gate: sample spread must read as a book, not a data dump.

## Automation opportunities and human review

Automate source ingestion, section segmentation, three-layer alignment checks, entity/anchor consistency reports, sitelen rendering, reusable layout, EPUB/PDF preflight and image/text-overflow detection. Human review remains mandatory for literary decisions, ambiguity, tone, source provenance/rights, cover, release territories, pricing and final KDP submission.

## Metadata / search posture

Use direct object language: `Jekyll and Hyde`, `Toki Pona`, `sitelen pona`, `parallel text`, `constructed language`, `classic`. Avoid claims of official language status, educational outcomes, horror-film affiliation, or a conventional scholarly translation.

## Known risks

- Toki Pona community may already contain an unindexed translation.
- The source's ambiguity and delayed revelation can be damaged by explanatory tooling.
- A premium three-layer object has a small market ceiling and expensive pagination.
- KDP marketplace/territory policy must be checked near release; this brief is not clearance.
