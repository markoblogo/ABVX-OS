Derived metrics: live title count, rolling 30d paid units/royalties, lifetime paid units/royalties, 30d royalty per live title, 30d first-submission slot count and royalty per slot, structural follow-on title count, KENP with its own window/status, and read-through for Book 1→2 and 2→3 when directly observable. Follow-on count means later existing line members, not evidence that production was causally prompted by a winner.

Use the latest canonical observed actual available on/before the requested date; never sum overlapping snapshots. A line total requires all members and comparable windows; currencies must be EUR for royalty totals. Output includes per-title windows, raw evidence references, statuses and snapshot dates. Read-through requires the existing DIRECT_MATCHED_COHORT contract, identified entry/continuation books, positive entrants, valid continuers, cohort basis and identity evidence. Aggregate sales ratios, free units, KENP totals and catalog membership are not matched readers. KU starts remain UNKNOWN unless observed; KENP is neither a paid purchase nor proof of completion.
