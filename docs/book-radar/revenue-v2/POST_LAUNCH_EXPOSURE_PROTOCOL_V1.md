# Post-Launch Exposure Protocol V1

D0–D7: organic baseline. At D7 or later, observed paid orders =0 plus weak/UNKNOWN organic exposure recommends a bounded paid-exposure test for owner approval. UNKNOWN paid orders or unknown age requires evidence collection first. UNKNOWN exposure is not zero demand.

Recommended paid test shape: exact or tightly relevant high-intent Amazon queries; small fixed **total** budget and observation period set by owner before action; record impressions, clicks, orders and spend with the same time window and attribution scope. No ads are started by this implementation. Budget, bids and duration remain unset; the owner approves a concrete bounded test separately.

Interpret NO_IMPRESSIONS, NO_CLICKS, CLICKS_NO_ORDERS and PAID_ORDERS separately. Missing instrumentation stays UNKNOWN; no clicks/orders are inferred from rank, listing, KENP or royalty. A paid-order signal prompts LINE_EXPLOIT_REVIEW_BEFORE_BROAD_DISCOVERY, not automatic extension. The review asks about adjacency, second-title cost, same-buyer relevance, cross-sell, seasonality and series economics; consider updates to already-submitted titles before spending another new-title slot.

The growth report treats stale paid snapshots conservatively and emits collection recommendations. Exposure/click instrumentation must identify the same `recent` window. Unknown age or incomparable instrumentation cannot be promoted into an observed funnel stage. The direct `exposure_review` API expects caller-supplied observed quantities and age with supporting evidence; it executes no external operation.

Good Dogs remains EXISTING SERIES_BET — ALREADY LAUNCHED and extension HOLD. Evidence review or a separately owner-approved exposure test does not change that production hold. Track paid units, EUR royalties, applicable KU/KENP, series 30d/lifetime totals and directly observed read-through; missing data stays UNKNOWN.

Audience infrastructure is not a discovery prerequisite. After meaningful paid signal, recommend a free bonus/reader kit → landing page → optional email capture → related title. “Meaningful” requires owner review of repeated paid activity, acquisition economics and audience relevance; a single purchase is a review prompt, not permission to build infrastructure. No landing page, bonus, email list or ad manager was created.
