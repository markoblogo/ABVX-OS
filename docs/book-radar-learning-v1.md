# MMR Retrospective & Learning Loop v1

Cost: NORMAL. Additive, local, deterministic MVP; no data migration, dependencies, new scheduler, scorer changes, network collector or Cortex participation.

## Audit and minimal design

The current research entrypoint validates browser receipts, uses the four existing manual adapters, imports into isolated run roots and retains outputs. `book_radar_markets` already validates missingness, proof hashes, formats and commercial gates. `book_radar_outcomes` already models paid/free/KENP, exposure, reporting windows and delayed outcomes. `process_learning._write_once` provides exclusive atomic, idempotent writes. Reuse these capabilities; an external research agent/framework is unnecessary for this offline stage.

The gap is persistent research-method learning. Analyst rankings are separate files, candidate labels can change, and three October 11 output directories include processing replays. Directory counts are not independent market observations or commercial trials. Existing outcome records cannot be attributed to a discovery hypothesis without an explicit binding. The saved Monday heartbeat exists; October 12's timing discrepancy remains unresolved and is not repaired by this feature.

Owner context as reported 2026-10-17: September KDP revenue approximately EUR76, mainly the existing Toki Pona catalogue; one Odyssey sale in the first half of October; nine previously proposed candidates without sufficient commercial confirmation. These are OWNER_REPORTED context, not imported verified sales, calibrated forecast outcomes or evidence of a sustained decline. Owner production estimate of 0.5–1 working day is an estimate, not measured effort. No manuscript or personal data leaves the local workspace; Cortex is outside this feature.

Store ignored, versioned records under `.tmp/multi-market-radar/learning`: immutable per-run snapshots, content-addressed proof blobs, immutable review reports, one identified improvement proposal and append-only validation/adoption events. Existing run directories and canonical outcomes stay read-only. Same run/content returns the existing snapshot; changed content under an already frozen run ID is rejected. The exact source observation identity deduplicates replays globally, even across run IDs. Different snapshots or dates do not imply independent commercial trials.

Despite the ignored `.tmp` location shared with existing Radar runs, this learning directory is persistent research history: exclude it from temporary-file cleanup and back it up with the source evidence. No automatic deletion/retention policy is introduced.

Hypothesis continuity uses explicit candidate ID, language and target geography. Preserve all original labels/assessments; flag changed buyer/job/context for review, rather than silently merging by similar topic. Source-level changes are compared only on the same direct product URL, market, language, geography, metric kind/unit and compatible period. Never compare representative search-result prices or different currencies/windows as a trajectory. Observation dates, campaign funding windows and own sales windows remain separate.

## Decisions and hold strategy

All book/digital-product production is on HOLD. HOLD/VALIDATE/GO are review recommendations, not execution authority. No jobs, launches or probabilistic percentages are generated. Expected financial return per production hour stays UNKNOWN without relevant outcome and effort evidence.

- HOLD: no usable relevant observation, missing buyer/job, or a supplied blocker.
- VALIDATE: concrete buyer/job and observed offer/activity with a specific missing-evidence question. This does not change existing independent qualification or market eligibility.
- GO: additionally requires the existing market eligibility gate, positive VERIFIED product-specific paid units in a known reporting window, and an explicitly source-bound completed commercial review of buyer, advantage, acquisition route and economics. A generic course count, review, shop total, BSR or pledge cannot substitute. This conservative display projection is tested before use; it does not loosen the existing scorer. The owner still decides and production remains blocked.

Optional `commercial-review.json` binds its review to the research digest and candidate ID. Each required facet cites retained proof hashes; declarations are review assertions, not automated proof of business truth. Unbound/incomplete reviews never yield GO. Improvements to commercial criteria, data sources or the model remain proposed until documented validation and owner review; no automatic method adoption.

## Historical outcomes and learning

An explicit `outcome-links.json` maps a frozen hypothesis key to an existing versioned `book-radar-observed-actual/v1` ID and book ID. The adapter reuses outcome validation and verifies retained evidence before appending a bound observation. Never automatically join portfolio results by title/topic or rewrite canonical records. Unknown results, no exposure and not-yet-mature windows do not mean failure. Positive paid response supports that bounded observation; it does not prove commercial success or a forecast. Retrospective claims remain UNPROVEN unless an appropriate preregistered prediction/experiment can actually be adjudicated; v1 does not manufacture those contracts retrospectively.

One focused proposal per review: a T7 panel of the same direct products, formats and typed metrics, replacing additional breadth until missing decision evidence is addressed. Preregister three candidates/two products each, at most 20 research minutes; measure comparable follow-up coverage and newly resolved evidence questions, with zero proxy-to-paid substitutions. Historical replay can test matching/deduplication and estimate coverage; without T7 and paid outcomes it cannot validate commercial accuracy. Reuse an unresolved proposal instead of producing duplicate generic ideas.

Validation/adoption events are immutable, hash-bound and ordered by timestamp. Validation requires a declared dataset, measurements and retained proof. Synthetic technical tests cannot establish commercial-method validity. Adoption of a research/model/source change requires owner approval evidence and prior real-data validation; safe technical corrections require passing tests. Adoption events record status only; they never modify algorithms automatically.

## Integration and verification

The existing entrypoint runs learning after processing; pass the already prepared external ranking with `--ranking` when available. After the analyst finishes the report, `--learn-run <directory>` freezes a distinct final review including the ranking without changing an earlier snapshot. Historical backfill uses the same command and is explicitly a replay, never fresh acquisition. `--learn-history` ingests existing research chronologically. The same heartbeat runs research, then this finalization; there is no second weekly process.

Tests cover immutable/idempotent history, replay deduplication, ID/context changes, unit/window/product comparability, proxy boundaries, delayed paid/unknown/zero outcomes, guarded decisions and improvement transitions. Run existing provider/outcome/revenue checks plus the historical replay. Compare all old proof/state hashes before/after. A successful replay proves learning behavior only; it does not prove fresh browser collection, punctual scheduling, commercial calibration or publication readiness.

## Local interfaces

All modes use `scripts/run_multi_market_radar.py`; use `--history <isolated-root>` for tests. `--learn-run <completed-run>` freezes source and assessment stages; `--learn-history` backfills retained completed runs. Reports retain implementation/source hashes as well as version strings. Snapshot, proposal, event, outcome and report records use `schemas/book_radar_learning.schema.json`, self-digests and atomic exclusive writes. Proof blobs retain exact original bytes in hash-checked base64 envelopes. This detects accidental edits; local filesystem ownership remains the trust boundary.

`--learning-event <json> [--evidence-base <root>]` appends an event with `proposal_id: matched-product-t7-v1`, `experiment_id`, and `status`. Register first with `VALIDATION_REGISTERED` and a concrete `plan`. A result also needs `dataset_kind: REAL_HISTORY|SYNTHETIC`, `measurements`, and `evidence: [{path, sha256}]`. `VALIDATED` requires the real T7 coverage measurements `panel_size: 6`, `matched_followups: >=4`, `questions_resolved: >=1`, `research_minutes: <=20`, `proxy_to_paid_substitutions: 0`, `minimum_followup_days: >=7`. Otherwise record `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` or `REJECTED`. `ADOPTED` additionally requires a previous validation for the same experiment and an `owner_approval: {path, sha256}`. Proof provenance and semantic conclusions remain review responsibilities; a status assertion cannot independently prove commercial truth. No event executes adoption.

`--bind-outcomes <links.json> --actuals <state.json> --evidence-base <root>` reads `links: [{hypothesis_key, actual_id, book_id}]`. Hypothesis keys are explicit `candidate-id|language|geography` values from the frozen report. Bind only reviewed identities; unknown/different book identities or legacy unversioned actuals are rejected. New actual IDs or corrected snapshots append history; repeated identical content is idempotent. Original source windows and exposure states remain intact.

Optional `commercial-review.json` has `research_digest` and `candidates: {<id>: {facets: {buyer, paid_demand, advantage, channel, economics}}}`. Each facet has `conclusion: SUPPORTED`, a concrete `reason` and `proof_sha256` from the candidate's retained observations. This is an auditable analyst review, not a bypass for the original provider eligibility gates or dated paid-demand requirement. Global production HOLD remains in every report, including GO proposals.

## Prospective discovery-method comparison v1 — owner approved 2026-10-18

The existing module, journal, immutable proof store and executable now accept scoped method events. Historical snapshots, canonical outcomes, scoring and the six-product T7 contract are unchanged. `config/radar-method-profiles-v1.json` contains versioned birth/stop predicates for PAID_CHOICE_DEFECT, REPEATED_UNMET_JOB and the later commercial-market-first/object-audience challengers. Source marketplace is not a method. Pleasure, identity, gifting and aesthetics are eligible motives; no universal utilitarian-job requirement is introduced.

Use the **same** executable:

```sh
python3 scripts/run_multi_market_radar.py --method-event <event.json> --evidence-base <local-proof-directory>
python3 scripts/run_multi_market_radar.py --method-report
```

Events append under the existing `learning/events/`; `validation_scope` distinguishes METHOD_COMPARISON and PRODUCT_OUTCOME. T7 events retain their existing proposal and validation rules. Reports include a separate `method_comparison` projection. This is an offline record/validation interface, not a collector, pipeline, scheduler or product executor. Use one local operator at a time; parallel journal mutation is outside this bounded contract.

1. REGISTER_PROTOCOL freezes block ID, market/geography/language/format cohort, complete selected profile definitions, common qualifier, context/blinding limitations, method-level forecast or UNKNOWN, and arm/review/card caps. Reusing a method ID with a different definition is rejected. Do not infer blindness from a fresh thread. Preserve market and method effects as separate dimensions.
2. START_ARM records the actual local clock before research. FREEZE_HYPOTHESIS records stable niche/hypothesis IDs, buyer/payer, unmet need **or motive**, substitutes, supply-gap cause, product, encounter route, source proofs, negative evidence, birth/demand assessments, uncertainty, costs, next falsification, and a forecast metric/basis/horizon/range/rationale anchored to PUBLICATION_DATE. UNKNOWN ranges remain NOT_FORECASTABLE, never zero predictions. These are frozen before commercial outcomes and common review. Protocol-level questions are frozen before acquisition; candidate hypotheses arise during research, not retroactively from sales.
3. LINK_DISCOVERY records another method's INDEPENDENT_REDISCOVERY or CORROBORATION while that arm is open. Only the former receives discovery membership. One shared hypothesis/trial remains one observation, not two successes. Identity matching is an explicit analyst review; hashes cannot establish semantic equivalence or independence. Separate aliases require an explicit common niche/product-family identity and must never reset the trial cap.
4. CLOSE_ARM retains all searches, source failures and limitations, then records actual elapsed seconds. Late closes remain visible as OVERRUN; new cards after the cap are rejected. Arms run sequentially. START_REVIEW requires all arms closed; CLOSE_REVIEW freezes findings and proof, with actual time and any overrun. No post-review candidate insertion or forecast edits. New contexts need a new prospective block. Keep the total research resource including failed accesses, not only productive page reads.
5. REGISTER_TRIAL is **a record only**, never permission/execution: it requires a separately owner-approved bounded plan, identity proofs, a future publication time, and full costs or UNKNOWN. Maximum three initial product families per stable niche across blocks; alternate formats/revisions cannot count as fresh trials. Production currently remains HOLD, so do not register/run any operational product experiment. BIND_OUTCOME reuses the existing versioned KDP actual/book validators, retained proofs and `outcomes.calibrate`/`classify`; requires exact identity, verified dates, prospective registration and explicit organic-attribution state. Other markets stay unsupported for own outcomes until a real authorized export adapter exists. Do not map reviews/students/pledges into KDP paid units.

Read-only comparison separates method version × market/context × block. It reports hypothesis/demand-gate coverage, registered and observed launches, products with scope-matched paid response, shared membership, original typed income windows, costs/UNKNOWN, and forecast calibration. KDP US paperback comparability currently requires AMAZON_COM scope and a sole evidenced paperback format; account-wide or multi-format totals remain INCOMPARABLE. Incomplete/future windows and unknown forecasts are not failures; cumulative windows cannot be summed. Repeated niche response is retained for manual adjudication from at least two closed nonoverlapping 30-day positive-paid windows; this is a proposed operational criterion, not an empirically calibrated success threshold. No automatic niche-success label, method winner, allocation change, score update or production authorization is emitted. Counts of candidates or first orders cannot select a winner. Full time/cost denominators and organic attribution remain separate; no ads alone is not verified organic acquisition.

Method learning binds only prospectively registered hypotheses. Historical Toki outcomes can be separately discussed as controls but never backfilled as a new method's successes. Market qualification stays separate from the later owner capability/portfolio overlay. A future comparison must use equivalent mature sales horizons and explicit revenue/cost bases; descriptive differences are not causal superiority. Future blocks may select the two preserved challenger profiles without introducing another execution path.
