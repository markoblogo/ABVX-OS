# Bracken Ford Full Draft Learning Delta V1

Taxonomy: observation / inference / hypothesis / practice

## Observation

Anchors-first drafting exposed the missing causal setup for the final safety incident. The Finch/contractor/site-access sequence made the bag event a consequence rather than a thematic import. The old eight-story review manuscript was useful as mechanism evidence but could not serve as the canonical novel source.

## Inference

Illustrations can be produced before final prose only when they remain replaceable semantic assets. Their final placement must be recalculated against the completed manuscript; early review pagination must not govern the novel.

## Hypothesis

A causal architecture can absorb selected standalone mechanisms while preserving a linked town-world, but the middle requires explicit scene-level pressure to avoid collapsing back into a collection.

## Practice

Keep development prose, review artifacts, and illustration placement in separate versioned files. Run anchor, causality, continuity, style, provenance, and product-fit gates before human editorial work. Do not make sales or comprehension claims from this single experiment.
